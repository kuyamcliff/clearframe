## ClipFX Enhance 1.1.4 (Windows)

### One activity log you can send when something goes wrong
- **Everything is recorded from the moment the app opens**, in one file in your **Documents** folder: `ClipFX Enhance Log.txt`.
  - Startup: app version, Windows/macOS/Linux version, WebView version, folders, settings, CPU, RAM and every GPU found (or why none was usable).
  - Every click, menu choice, setting change and keyboard shortcut in the window, and every action the window asks the engine to do, with how long it took and whether it failed.
  - Errors and crashes, including from the processing workers, with full details.
  - Job progress (frames, speed, time left), model downloads, previews and updates.
  - Performance every 15 seconds: CPU, memory, GPU use and temperature, plus a warning whenever the window freezes.
- Find it with **Help → Show log file**, or in **Preferences → Diagnostics**.
- The log stays on your computer. Typed text and passwords are never recorded. It trims itself when it passes 50 MB.

**Windows only.** If you already have ClipFX Enhance, the update bar in the app downloads just the app update (about 8 MB) and restarts. You don't need to download the full installer again. The full installer `ClipFX-Enhance-windows-x64-setup.exe` is only for new installs.

macOS and Linux stay on 1.1.3; the macOS and Linux downloads on this page are the 1.1.3 builds.
