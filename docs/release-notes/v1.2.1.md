## ClipFX Enhance 1.2.1 (Windows)

### Enhancement no longer makes real-life video look worse
Turning on **Enhancement** used the anime model, which smooths faces, skin, hair and clothes into a painted look. On good-quality video, the result could look worse than the original.
- Enhancement now starts with **Real-ESRGAN General v3**, the model made for real-life footage. Anime models are still there for animation, and the anime presets keep using them.
- **Reduce noise** is now on **Auto**: the app measures how grainy your video is and sets it for you. Clean video keeps all its texture; grainy video gets cleaned up. Untick Auto to set it yourself.
- Fixed: the Reduce noise slider could show 50% while the strongest setting (100%) was being applied.
- **Strength** below 100% blends with a sharper resize than before.

Tested on YouTube-style 240p, 360p, 480p and 720p video against the full-quality original: the new default came out closer to the original than the old one at every size.

If you already have ClipFX Enhance, the update bar downloads just the app update (a few MB) and restarts. macOS and Linux stay on 1.1.3 for now.
