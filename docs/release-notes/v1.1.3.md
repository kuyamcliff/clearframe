## ClipFX Enhance 1.1.3

### Opens ready to work
- **FFmpeg is ready at once.** The app remembers the FFmpeg it found and uses it immediately on the next launch, checking quietly in the background in case it changed. No more "Checking FFmpeg…".
- **Videos open faster.** A video appears as soon as it is read; the scan for interlacing runs afterwards and only then suggests deinterlacing.
- **The export summary appears with everything else** instead of seconds later: video details are read once and reused, and hardware is detected once in the background at launch.
- The window opens in the app's own colours, without a light flash first.

Windows and Linux (.deb) users of 1.1.x get this through the update bar in the app; on Windows only the app itself is downloaded (about 8 MB). On macOS, the AppImage and the .rpm, download the new version from this page.
