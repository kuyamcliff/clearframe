## ClipFX Enhance 1.1.2

### Faster, more reliable startup
- The window now appears straight away. FFmpeg is detected in the background; the top bar shows "Checking FFmpeg…" until it is ready, and anything that needs FFmpeg in the first seconds waits for it instead of failing.
- **Linux**: the window no longer waits for the desktop's colour-scheme service (xdg-desktop-portal). Where that service was slow or did not answer, the window appeared late or not at all. With the theme set to "System", light or dark now follows your GTK theme; choose Light or Dark in Preferences to override.

Windows and Linux (.deb) users of 1.1.x get this through the update bar in the app; on Windows only the app itself is downloaded (about 8 MB). On macOS, the AppImage and the .rpm, download the new version from this page.
