## ClipFX Enhance 1.2.0 (Windows)

### AI models now work on laptop and integrated GPUs
Exports could stop with "GPU did not finish … submission index …". Windows resets the graphics driver when one piece of GPU work runs longer than about two seconds, and a whole frame tile on a laptop GPU easily did.
- Work is now split into short pieces sized to your GPU's measured speed, so the driver is never left waiting.
- If the driver resets anyway, the app reopens the GPU and carries on; if the GPU still cannot run it, the export finishes on the CPU instead of failing.
- Failed exports explain what happened in plain words and what to do next; the technical details go to the log file (Help → Show log file).

### Color & light
The colour section is now a full grade: **Exposure, Brightness, Contrast, Shadows, Highlights, Gamma, Temperature, Tint, Saturation, Vibrance and Hue**. Double-click any slider to reset it, or use **Reset** for all. Grading also works with HDR → SDR and SDR → HDR.

### Sharpen
A new **Sharpen** section: **Adaptive** brings out fine detail without halos; **Unsharp mask** is stronger and has a radius. It runs at the final size, after resizing.

### Works with every kind of video
Tested on real footage in 20 formats (phone, camera, DVD, web, editing, HDR):
- Interlaced DVD and camcorder video is deinterlaced automatically before AI steps.
- Odd-sized videos export (trimmed by one pixel) instead of being refused.
- HDR videos stay HDR, saved in 10-bit HEVC automatically.
- MKV files with subtitles longer than the video export correctly.
- "4K" and "1080p" give exact standard sizes (3840×2160, 1920×1080) and wide films fit inside them.

### Windows
- Exports are not slowed down by Windows efficiency mode, and the PC does not go to sleep mid-export.
- Export progress on the taskbar button; it flashes when an export finishes in the background.
- Opening a video while ClipFX is running opens it in the same window.
- Menus open when you point at them.
- Errors are explained in plain words with what to do next.

### Diagnostics
To fix problems faster, ClipFX sends its own log (no videos, file names or personal data) in the background, also after being offline. Turn it off in Preferences → General → Privacy.

### Smoother interface
- Buttons respond when pressed; dialogs, menus, panels and sections ease in; sliders react under the pointer. Reduced motion turns it all off.
- New app icon and logo.
- Long file names no longer run under the view buttons.

If you already have ClipFX Enhance, the update bar downloads just the app update and restarts; you don't need the full installer again. The full installer `ClipFX-Enhance-windows-x64-setup.exe` is for new installs. macOS and Linux stay on 1.1.3 for now; their downloads on this page are the 1.1.3 builds.
